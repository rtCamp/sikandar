# Privacy Policy

Sikandar is an iOS and iPadOS score-keeping app published by rtCamp Inc. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

_Last updated: 2026-09-24_

## Summary

Sikandar does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no server-side component. Everything happens on your device.

## Data Handled by the App

- **Player names.** Names you type in are stored locally on your device in the app's database. They are not transmitted to rtCamp or any third party.
- **Games, rounds and scores.** Recorded locally on your device in the same database. Scores are abstract points; the app does not handle payments or real money.
- **App preferences.** Stored locally on your device.

This data is included in your device's normal iCloud or computer backup, under the same protections as the rest of your backup. Sikandar does not sync data between devices itself.

## Sharing

The Share button creates an image of a finished game (scorecard, settlement and rounds) and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app or recipient there. rtCamp never receives the image.

## Network Access

The app makes no network requests.

## Deleting Your Data

Deleting a game in History removes it and its rounds. Removing a player hides them from new games; their history stays until those games are deleted. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new "Last updated" date.
